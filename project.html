Roadmap

Planned work is tracked in ROADMAP.md. Items there are plans, not features: nothing on the roadmap is described on this site as working until it ships and is covered by the experiment suite.

For what v0.1 deliberately does not do today, see Limitations.

Proposals that change what counts as evidence go through an architecture decision record first, because the evidence model is the project's core contract.

Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome. make verify runs everything CI runs without a cluster; make demo-up && make test-e2e runs the experiment suite.
